# Planned student validation protocol

Status: **not conducted**. Proposed first pilot:5–10 consenting local students after curriculum review. No outcomes or participant counts are asserted.

Explain the prototype and its limits before participation. Participation is optional; use a non-identifying participant code and record consent/date in a separate observation sheet. Do not collect Aadhaar, caste, religion, phone numbers or contact details through the prototype. Tell the participant that browser data may be visible to another person using the same device, and clear it at the end. Arrange institution/guardian processes where applicable rather than improvising them inside the app.

Ask the participant to complete a profile, explain the three component scores in their own words, open a career, create a plan, complete/reopen a task, refresh and clear saved data. Record elapsed time without prompting first; note where assistance was needed. Do not treat automated completion times as newcomer usability evidence.

Five interview questions:

1. How do you currently decide what skill to learn next for a career?
2. Which device, time or cost limitation most affects your study?
3. What do you think the displayed coverage and exploration scores mean?
4. Which task in this plan feels feasible or infeasible this week, and why?
5. What information or support would make the next step more useful?

Record profile completion, seconds to first roadmap, explanation comprehension using a predefined checklist, usefulness1–5, working resource links, and self-reported task completion at an agreed follow-up. Preserve denominators, dates, missing responses and withdrawals. Compare device constraints without inferring protected attributes. Report qualitative limitations and sample size alongside any aggregate; do not claim causal employment impact.

Curriculum review should check required levels, skill weights, prerequisite mappings, phone alternatives, local equipment/training access, and the suitability of foreign documentation for introductory study. Changes require matching/planner regression tests. Estimate staff time and actual hosting usage before claiming a per-student delivery cost.
